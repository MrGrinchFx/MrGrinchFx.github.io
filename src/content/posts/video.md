Just copy the embed code from YouTube or other platforms, and paste it in the markdown file.

```yaml
---
title: There is no place like home
published: 2023-10-19
// ...
---

<iframe width="560" height="315" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/5tl_e-sJPRY?si=SAHY86zukprtL1Or" title="YouTube video player" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe>```
